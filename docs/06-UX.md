# 06 — UI/UX documentation

## Who and what

| User | Job | Success looks like |
|------|-----|--------------------|
| Host | Set up a tournament fast, stop running the bracket by hand, settle disputes, pay winners | Created in under a minute; one tap to pay |
| Player | Know the prize pool is real, pay, report a result, get paid | Pays once, sees their match, uploads one screenshot |
| Judge | Run the whole flow from a link in minutes | No setup, understands the value in the first screen |

Context: most players are on phones with patchy connections, so design mobile-first (360px), keep pages light, and make every action recoverable on refresh.

## Experience principles

1. **Show the money and who holds it.** The prize pool is the most prominent number on a tournament page, and the page says it is held by the platform until the host confirms the payout.
2. **Make the agent legible.** Every agent action appears in plain sentences in the activity feed. The agent never acts silently.
3. **One decision per screen.** Disputes and payout confirmation each present one clear choice with the evidence beside it.
4. **Irreversible actions are slow on purpose.** Only payout confirmation gets an extra confirm step, and it says plainly that the money cannot be recalled.
5. **Say what happened and what to do next** in every state, including failure.

## Information architecture

```
/                      landing, judge quick start
/login  /register
/play                  browse open tournaments            (player)
/play/t/:id            join, pay, my match                 (player)
/host                  my tournaments                      (host)
/host/new              create tournament                   (host)
/host/t/:id/payout     review and confirm payout           (host)
/host/t/:id/disputes/:d  resolve a dispute                 (host)
/t/:id                 public tournament page: bracket, players, activity, ledger
```

The public tournament page is the shared home for everyone; role-specific actions appear as a panel above the tabs.

## Core flows

### Host: create

1. Landing, "Try as host" (or log in). Host dashboard.
2. "New tournament". Two ways in, one result:
   - Describe it: a text box with an example ("8-player football knockout, $5 entry, winner 70%, runner-up 30%"). Submit shows a parsed preview.
   - Fill the form: title, game name, entry fee, winner and runner-up split.
3. Review card shows the final terms: players 8, entry fee, projected pool ($40), payouts ($28.00 and $12.00), and the line "Terms lock once you open registration."
4. "Open registration". Tournament page, state Open, with a share link.

### Player: join and pay

1. Open the tournament link, see pool and terms. "Join for $5".
2. Form: gamertag (the name that appears in your game) and PayPal email (sandbox personal account for this demo).
3. Redirect to PayPal, approve, return to the tournament page with "You're in" banner and your slot.
4. If cancelled: banner "Payment cancelled. Your slot is free again. Join again whenever you're ready."

### Play and report

1. When the bracket is drawn, the player's panel shows "Your match: Round 1 vs <opponent>".
2. After playing: "Upload your result screenshot" with preview and replace option until the match is evaluated.
3. Status line under the match: "Waiting for <opponent>'s screenshot", "Umpra is checking both screenshots", "Confirmed: you won 3-1", or "Under host review".
4. Winners see their next match appear automatically.

### Agent loop, as the user sees it

Activity feed updates as events happen: locked, bracket drawn, screenshots read, match confirmed, next round opened. No refresh needed (poll every 5 seconds on the tournament page; pause when the tab is hidden).

### Host: resolve a dispute

1. Banner on the tournament page: "1 match needs your decision". Opens the dispute view.
2. Dispute view: reason label, the agent's short summary, both screenshots side by side (tap to enlarge), extracted names and scores per screenshot, then two buttons: "<Player A> won" and "<Player B> won", and an optional note.
3. After resolving: "Resolved. Umpra has moved <winner> to the next round."

### Host: release the payout

1. After the final, banner: "Final confirmed. Review the payout."
2. Payout review: champion and runner-up, amounts, total equals the pool, PayPal email of each recipient, and a compact trail ("7 matches confirmed, 1 dispute resolved by you").
3. "Confirm payout" opens a dialog: "This sends $40.00 to 2 PayPal accounts. Payments cannot be recalled." Buttons: "Send payout" and "Go back".
4. Result: progress per recipient, then a receipt with PayPal batch id. Refreshing returns to the same state.

## Screens

For every screen: loading (skeleton), empty, and error states are required.

**Landing.** One sentence of what Umpra does, the lifecycle shown as the real sequence of steps (Describe, Pay, Play, Umpire, Pay out), two buttons "Try as host" and "Try as player", and a panel "Sandbox payments: use this test buyer" with test credentials when `DEMO_MODE=true`. The memorable element is a live mini bracket drawing itself once on load.

**Login / Register.** Role chosen at registration with one line explaining each role. Inline errors. Nothing else.

**Host dashboard.** List of the host's tournaments with state pill, pool, slots filled. Empty: "No tournaments yet. Create your first one."

**Create tournament.** Describe tab and Form tab, review card, "Open registration".

**Tournament page.** Header: title, game, state pill. Prize plate: pool amount, "held by Umpra until the host confirms", "$X of $40 collected" with 8 slot markers. Role panel (join, my match, resolve disputes, review payout). Tabs: Bracket, Players, Activity, Ledger. Bracket is the default tab once locked; Players is the default while open.

**Bracket.** Three columns (rounds) on desktop; on mobile, a round selector with one round at a time plus a "full bracket" horizontally scrollable view. Match card: two names, score once confirmed, status (waiting, in play, checking, needs host, confirmed). Winner's connector line draws into the next slot when confirmed.

**Players.** Gamertag, paid status, and (host only) PayPal email.

**Activity.** The officials' report: timestamped agent and user events as sentences, newest first. Agent events marked with the agent glyph, user events with the person's name.

**Ledger.** Table of money in (each entry fee, status) and money out (each payout, status), plus a total line. Refunds appear as their own rows.

**Dispute view, Payout review.** As in the flows above.

| locked | Full | pitch |
| in_progress | In play | pitch |

## Copy rules

Sentence case. Active voice. Buttons say what happens: "Join for $5", "Upload screenshot", "Confirm payout". The same action keeps the same name everywhere (the button "Confirm payout" leads to the toast "Payout confirmed"). Errors say what happened and how to fix it, with no apology and no blame. Plain words for users (say "checkout", not "order"; "screenshot", not "evidence"). No exclamation marks except real success on payout.

Key strings:

| Moment | Copy |
|--------|------|
| Pool caption | "Held by Umpra until the host confirms the payout." |
| Terms lock | "Terms lock once you open registration." |
| Join cancelled | "Payment cancelled. Your slot is free again." |
| Tournament full at capture | "This tournament filled up while you were paying. We've refunded $5.00 to your PayPal." |
| Waiting on opponent | "Waiting for <opponent> to submit their screenshot." |
| Checking | "Umpra is checking both screenshots." |
| Dispute banner (host) | "1 match needs your decision." |
| Unreadable upload | "We couldn't read that screenshot. Upload one that shows the final score and both player names." |
| Payout dialog | "This sends $40.00 to 2 PayPal accounts. Payments cannot be recalled." |
| Payout done | "Paid. $28.00 to <winner>, $12.00 to <runner-up>." |
| Payout partial failure | "<gamertag>'s payout failed: <reason>. The other payout went through. Retry this one." |
| Empty activity | "Nothing yet. Umpra will log every action here." |

## Responsive and accessibility

- Design at 360px first; test at 360, 768, 1280. No horizontal page scroll (the full-bracket view scrolls inside its own container).
- Touch targets at least 44px. Inputs have visible labels, not placeholders alone.
- Keyboard: logical tab order, visible focus ring, dialogs trap focus and return it on close, Esc closes.
- Colour is never the only signal: pills carry text, dispute state has an icon and label.
- Contrast at least 4.5:1 for text (tokens in `07` are chosen for this).
- Screenshots have alt text ("<gamertag>'s submitted screenshot").
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: the bracket line appears instantly instead of drawing.
- Live updates announce through a polite `aria-live` region (for example "Match confirmed").
- Slow connections: skeletons not spinners, images lazy-loaded and constrained (uploads resized client-side to a max of 1600px on the long edge before sending).
